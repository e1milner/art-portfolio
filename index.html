About

Zack Kelly is a comic artist and illustrator from Massachusetts. He began drawing as a little kid, inspired by cartoons and superheroes. When Zack delved into comics, he was blown away by the storytelling. Inspired by this comic and the cartoons he watched as a kid, Zack began creating compelling art and stories designed to have a positive effect on people.
